# HOME / PIECE 02 — Reference Card

## Status
ACTIVE — second Home content piece only.

## Primary real-world reference
Rossano Ferretti — Our Salons
https://www.rossanoferretti.com/salons/

The live page was inspected directly before implementation.

## Real design characteristics observed
First editorial section after the hero:
- pure white background;
- dark grey / black text;
- large elegant serif statement;
- smaller clean sans-serif supporting line;
- single centered column;
- no image in this section;
- no CTA;
- no border/rule;
- very generous vertical whitespace;
- luxury is created through restraint, typography and scale rather than effects.

## What SEZIONE adapts
- white field immediately after the image-heavy hero;
- serif/sans contrast;
- centered typographic composition;
- significant empty space around the statement;
- no CTA;
- no decorative graphic layer;
- a quieter rhythm after the high-impact monochrome hero.

## What SEZIONE does NOT copy
- Rossano Ferretti name or logo;
- exact wording;
- exact font family;
- exact salon claims;
- exact measurements or layout coordinates.

## SEZIONE content
Headline:
“NON PARTIAMO DAL TAGLIO. / PARTIAMO DA TE.”

Supporting copy:
“Forma, texture, abitudini e manutenzione danno la direzione. Il risultato non parte da un modello standard, ma da come vuoi vivere i tuoi capelli ogni giorno.”

This is concept positioning, not a verified operational claim about a real salon.

## Image-source plan
No image is used in this content piece because the real primary reference also relies on a pure typography interlude.

The next image-based piece must again research real photography before any generated asset is considered.

## Scope

No services grid, team, salon interior, booking or footer is part of this approval cycle.
