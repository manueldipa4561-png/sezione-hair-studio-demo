## Primary real-world reference
George Northwood
https://www.georgenorthwood.com/

Official site inspected directly before implementation.

## What the real site does
Header:
- pure white background;
- black uppercase navigation;
- centered bold sans-serif wordmark;
- sparse navigation with generous whitespace;
- restrained utility treatment.

Hero:
- full-bleed monochrome editorial portrait;
- strong black/white contrast;
- oversized uppercase sans-serif typography over the image;
- generous scale rather than decorative UI;
- no obvious hero button competing with the photography;
- visual hierarchy is created by image + type, not effects.

## What SEZIONE adapts
- black / white as the first-piece palette;
- sparse white header;
- centered wordmark on desktop;
- uppercase, high-impact sans-serif hero type;
- full-bleed monochrome photography;
- minimal interface chrome;
- strong image-to-text relationship.

## What SEZIONE does NOT copy
- George Northwood name, logo or brand language;
- exact navigation wording;
- exact hero copy;
- exact typeface;
- salon-specific claims;
- exact composition coordinates.
